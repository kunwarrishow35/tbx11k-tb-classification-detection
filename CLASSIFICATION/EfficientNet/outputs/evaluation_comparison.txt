9. FACTUAL OBSERVATIONS & DATA CHARACTERISTICS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Obsolete TB Sample Size: Obsolete Pulmonary Tuberculosis has only 17 validation
   positives and 24 test positives, which causes empirical threshold sweeps and
   resulting F1 scores to be sensitive to small shifts in predictions.
2. Metric Trade-Off: At validation-tuned thresholds, Macro-F1 increases from 0.4470
   to 0.6039 while subset Exact-Match Accuracy decreases from 96.09% to 82.61%.
3. Obsolete TB Precision Trade-Off: Lowering the Obsolete threshold from 0.50 to
   0.0600 increases true positive detections from 0 to 22 (out of 24), but introduces
   98 false positives, resulting in an Obsolete precision of 0.1833.
4. Cross-Class Categorical Migration: In the 4-category mapping under tuned
   thresholds, 117 test images are assigned to 'Both' (95 from true Active_Only,
   20 from true Obsolete_Only, and 2 from true Both), reflecting simultaneous
   positive predictions on both output channels.
5. Metric Invariance: ROC-AUC and PR-AUC values are calculated directly on
   continuous probabilities and remain strictly invariant across all classification
   threshold choices (Active ROC-AUC: 0.9807, PR-AUC: 0.7944; Obsolete ROC-AUC: 0.9225,
   PR-AUC: 0.1972).
